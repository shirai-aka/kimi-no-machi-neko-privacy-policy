君の街ねこ (Kimi no Machi Neko)

Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

This app does not know who you are.

君の街ねこ (Kimi no Machi Neko; the “App”) is an app for collecting the local cats of the town you are in. It uses location information for that purpose, but your location is used only to find out “which municipality you are in right now”, and only at the moment you press “さがす” (Search). It does not track your movements. This page summarizes the information the App handles and how to contact us.

1. What We Do Not Collect

  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • Location information is not sent to the developer. The location at the time you press “さがす” (Search) is converted into a municipality on the device. Latitude and longitude are never sent to the developer’s server or to advertising companies (the developer does not have a server).
  • Location is not obtained in the background. While the App is closed, location information is not used at all.
  • The developer does not analyze your usage. No analytics tool for the developer is built into the App.

2. Location Information

  • Location information is obtained only once, when you press “さがす” (Search), and the prefecture and municipality of that place (or the country, outside Japan) is determined on the device. The country is determined using border data included in the App.
  • Records of the cats you have met (date and time, prefecture, municipality; or the country name when you searched outside Japan) are stored only on your device and in your iCloud (see below). Latitude and longitude are not recorded (latitude and longitude recorded by earlier versions are erased at launch after updating).
  • To decide which cats appear depending on the weather, an approximate location rounded to about 1 km is sent to Apple’s weather service (WeatherKit).
  • Location permission can be revoked at any time from the iPhone’s Settings. Revoking it does not erase your collection or records.
  • The speed of movement at the time the location is obtained (used to determine cats you can meet on a Shinkansen and the like) is also only used on the device, and is neither recorded nor transmitted.
  • If you enter your birthday in Settings, only the month and day are stored on the device (used to determine cats you can meet only on your birthday). It is not synced to iCloud and is not sent to the developer. You can erase it from Settings at any time.
  • The “ひとことメモ” (short note) you write in a record is stored, together with the record of the encounter, only on your device and in your iCloud. It is not sent to the developer.
  • If you turn on “特別な日のお知らせ” (Special-day notifications), the notification is only scheduled on the device, and nothing is sent outside.
  • To display the home screen and lock screen widgets, the name, town name and picture of the cat you met most recently are placed in the App’s private area on the device (App Group). They are not sent outside the device.

3. iCloud Sync

Records of the cats you have met are synced between your devices signed in with the same Apple account through Apple’s iCloud (the CloudKit private database). This data is stored on Apple’s servers tied to your account, and third parties, including the developer, cannot access it. If you do not use iCloud, records are stored only on the device.

4. Weather

Some cats appear depending on the weather at the time. Apple’s WeatherKit is used to determine the weather. When this happens, your current location is sent to Apple in order to obtain the weather. Apple’s handling of this is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive this information.

5. Advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads). For ad delivery and measurement, Google may collect the following information.

  • Device ID (the advertising identifier; only if you chose “Allow” in the iPhone’s tracking permission)
  • Product interaction (such as ad views and taps)
  • Advertising data (information about the ads shown)
  • Coarse location (a region estimated from the IP address; the location information obtained by the App is not passed on)
  • Crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data

For details, please see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s explanation of advertising. Even if you do not allow tracking, you can use all features of the App (in that case, non-personalized ads are shown). You can change the permission at any time from the iPhone’s “Settings” → “Privacy & Security” → “Tracking”.

6. In-App Purchases

The in-app purchase “広告を消す” (Remove ads) hides the banner ads. The purchase is made through Apple’s system, and the developer does not receive your payment information.

7. Data Storage and Deletion

Records and settings are carried over to a new device through iCloud sync or the iPhone’s standard backup. This is your own data, and the developer cannot access it. Records can be erased at any time from the App’s “設定” (Settings) → “記録をすべて削除” (Delete all records) (after confirmation, records on your other devices using the same iCloud are erased as well; the “広告を消す” (Remove ads) purchase remains). If you delete the App from the device, the data on the device is erased. Synced data on iCloud can also be deleted from the iPhone’s “Settings” → Apple Account → “iCloud” → “Manage Account Storage”.

8. Data Sources

  • Administrative areas are determined using data derived from the “National Land Numerical Information (Administrative Area Data)” (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, Japan).
  • Countries are determined using data derived from Natural Earth border data (public domain).
  • Weather data: Apple Weather (attribution is shown on the App’s settings screen).

9. Support & FAQ

  • Contact: shirai.apps@ymail.ne.jp (please include your iPhone model, iOS version, App version and what happened)
  • No cat appears — Even if you search in the same place, you are not guaranteed to meet one. When none is found, a paw print is left, and once you have three paw prints you are guaranteed to meet a cat on the next “さがす” (Search). In a prefecture you visit for the first time, you are guaranteed to meet that prefecture’s cat.
  • I can no longer press “さがす” (Search) — “さがす” can be used up to 3 times a day. In a municipality you have never been to, the first search does not use up a try. Watching an ad adds 3 more tries. The count resets at midnight every day.
  • What happens overseas? — There are cats for 34 countries and regions. The first time you press “さがす” (Search) in that country you are guaranteed to meet one, and that search does not use up a try. In other places, you can meet cats that are found everywhere (nationwide cats). Prefecture cats and town cats can only be met within Japan.
  • Town cats rarely appear — Town cats appear only when you search within a specific municipality. In the collection, the town name is shown under each town cat you have not yet met.
  • I am asked for location permission — It is used only at the moment you press “さがす” (Search), to find out which municipality you are in. It does not track your movements.
  • Will my records remain if I switch devices? — If you use iCloud with the same Apple account, they are carried over automatically.
  • I removed ads, but they are still shown — Please try “購入を復元” (Restore purchases) on the settings screen.
